# The team

## Tombatossals Softworks

Tombatossals Softworks is an independent software studio building original,
legally-clean, lovingly-engineered software for the hardware the industry has
written off. Castalia OS is its flagship project: a from-scratch desktop
operating system for early- and late-2000s PCs.

The studio's method is visible in the work — a written design "bible" before
the code, original artwork with a provenance ledger, and continuous-integration
gates that enforce the design system, the legal discipline and the performance
budget on every commit. Nothing borrowed that wasn't given freely; nothing
shipped that isn't tested.

## Creators

**Dave Abellán** — co-creator. Direction, systems and shell engineering, and
the build-and-verification pipeline behind Castalia OS.

**Claudio di Castello** — co-creator. Design, product and the visual language
of the Castalia desktop.

Together they set out to make the desktop they wished those old machines had
shipped with: fast on 512 MB of RAM, beautiful without a compositor, and
entirely their own.
